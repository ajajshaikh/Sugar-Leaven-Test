Est. small, baked honest

Bread has time. We give it that.

Sugar is a family-run bakery built around one idea: nothing here is rushed. Our sourdough proofs for a full day, our cakes are layered by hand, and every morning starts before sunrise so the ovens are ready when you are.

The Sugar way

Small batches. Real ingredients. No shortcuts.

Every loaf and cake that leaves our kitchen is made from four honest ingredients and a lot of patience. We mill what we can locally, retard our doughs overnight, and bake in small batches so nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting for you — it's made fresh, close to the moment you'll eat it.
