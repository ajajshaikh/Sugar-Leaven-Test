Welcome to Sugar Leaven

The Art of Fine Baking

At Sugar Leaven Patisserie, we believe that baking is more than a craft—it's a labor of love. Every loaf, every pastry, every cake that leaves our kitchen carries the warmth of tradition and the finesse of modern culinary artistry.

Nestled in the heart of Powai, Mumbai, our boutique bakery is dedicated to creating exceptional baked goods using time-honored techniques and the finest ingredients. From our signature sourdough loaves to our elaborate celebration cakes, each creation is a testament to our unwavering commitment to quality and taste.

Meet Our Chef

Aditi | Pastry Chef & Founder

Aditi's journey in pastry arts began with a deep passion for the transformative power of baking. Trained in classical European techniques, she brings a refined touch to every creation while honoring the warmth of home-style baking.

Her philosophy is simple: use exceptional ingredients, respect traditional methods, and infuse every recipe with genuine care. At Sugar Leaven, Aditi personally oversees every batch, ensuring that what reaches your table is nothing short of perfection.
