Our Story

Baked by hand, since day one

Sugar started in a home kitchen with one starter, one oven, and a lot of trial and error. It still runs on the same principle — do less, but do it properly.

Home/About
How it started

From a kitchen counter to a neighborhood bakery

Sugar began the way most honest bakeries do: with too much bread for one household to eat. What started as loaves shared with neighbors slowly became weekend orders, then a market stall, and eventually the shop you can visit today.

We kept the parts that mattered — the slow ferment, the small batches, the attention to a recipe until it's right — and built a proper kitchen around them. Nothing here is mass-produced, and that's a deliberate choice, not a limitation.

Today, Sugar is still run day-to-day by the family that started it, alongside a small team of bakers who share the same standard: if it isn't good enough to serve a friend, it doesn't leave the kitchen.

Head Chef
Meet the Chef

The chef behind every recipe

Owner & Head Pastry Chef

Sugar's kitchen is led by the owner's wife, a trained pastry chef whose approach shapes every recipe that leaves the ovens. Her background spans classic French pastry technique and years of hands-on sourdough development, and it shows in the precision behind even the simplest items on our counter.

She built the Sugar menu from the ground up — testing hydration levels, fermentation windows, and lamination methods until each recipe met a standard she was willing to put her name behind. That standard hasn't moved since.

Our Approach

Culinary journey & philosophy

Four ideas guide every recipe that makes it onto the Sugar counter.

Culinary Journey

Formal pastry training in classic French technique, followed by years spent developing natural fermentation methods for bread — a path that blends precision with patience.

Philosophy

Good baking is mostly restraint: fewer ingredients, more time, and the discipline not to rush a process that depends on waiting.

Baking Style

Naturally leavened, minimally processed, and built around seasonal ingredients sourced from small regional suppliers wherever possible.

Signature Specialties

Country levain loaves, laminated butter croissants, and made-to-order celebration cakes designed around each client's occasion.
